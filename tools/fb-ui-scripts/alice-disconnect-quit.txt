# disconnect-quit: Settings -> Network OFF and then leaving. Both paths call
# `Runtime.stopClient`, and closing the stop channel twice used to panic — so
# the assertion here is the run REACHING ITS END and printing UITEST PASS: the
# driver's own teardown calls stopClient a second time after the script has
# already disconnected.
#
# Driven by `wata-fb uitest` (uiscript.scala) — see that file's header.

family @bob:localhost
wait syncing 1 900
wait convs 1 900

# dot2 -> settings; item 3 is Network.
tap dot2
tap down
tap down
tap down
advance 5
tap enter
advance 30
checkpoint disconnect-off

# back to the wata applet and out through the quit edge: two-step, so both
# presses are needed. The scripted driver ignores the quit itself (the script
# says when a session ends) — what matters is the teardown that follows.
tap dot1
advance 2
tap back
advance 1
expect quitarm 1
tap back
advance 2
