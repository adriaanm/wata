# dm-roundtrip, phase 3: bob sees the DM arrive with an unplayed badge,
# opens it (read receipt -> played mark), plays it, and replies into the
# same canonical room.
#
# quiet, NOT the play default: notify priming happens on the first non-empty
# snapshot, so under load a backlog that syncs in across two snapshots turns
# its tail into an "arrival" — and play mode then auto-plays and RECEIPTS it,
# eating the badge this phase's goldens pin (and poisoning the receipt state
# every later phase reads). Quiet mode banners instead, and the banner is
# waited out below.
notifymode quiet

wait syncing 1 900
# [Family, Alice-DM]: the canonical room synced in with its net.wata.dm stamp
wait convs 2 900
tap down
wait msgs 1 2000
# gate on the badge itself, not just the message count: the checkpoint shows
# the unplayed badge on the Alice row, and `unplayed` is the summed count the
# badge renders from — selection-independent, so the shot cannot land on a
# frame where the arrival is countable but the badge state is not yet up.
# Then outlast the quiet banner a raced split-backlog arrival raises (~122
# frames of frame time); both waits are 0-frame no-ops on the common path.
wait unplayed 1 2000
waitmax notifybanner 0 200
advance 30
checkpoint dm-bob-roster

# open the DM, then OK on alice's message: it plays through the sim audio
# actor, and the receipt goes out when the audio ENDS — so the played mark
# turning on is proof the clip was heard, not that the screen was looked at.
tap enter
advance 30
tap enter
wait played 1 2000
advance 30
checkpoint dm-bob-played

# reply: PTT inside the conversation view sends into this DM room
key ptt press
advance 20
key ptt release
wait msgs 2 1500
advance 120
checkpoint dm-bob-reply
